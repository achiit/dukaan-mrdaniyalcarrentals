About us

Ten years on the roads of Bodhan

I started this business with one car and a simple idea: a customer should never have to worry about the vehicle. Over ten years, people from Rakasipet, Bodhan, Nizamabad and Rudrur have trusted us for weddings, hospital trips and long drives.

I still check every car myself before it goes out. If a car is not clean and running well, it does not leave. My business is named after my family, so every customer is treated like one of ours.

  • Well-maintained cars with strong engine performance
